Drumsheet for teachers

Write a groove, send it as homework. Your student opens one link (no app, no login) and practises with the groove to play along with, tempo steps to tick off and one clear goal per exercise.

Real screenshots with demo students.

How it works

  1. Write the groove, fill or rudiment in Drumsheet, like you already do.
  2. On your teacher page, press + Homework, paste the link and add a goal or a few notes. About two minutes per student.
  3. Your student opens their own page on their phone. It's live straight away and can be added to the home screen like an app.
