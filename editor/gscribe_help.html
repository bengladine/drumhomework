Beatsheet help

Free online drum notation editor. Everything you write is stored in the link, so bookmarking or sharing the link saves your groove.

Writing notes

  • Click a cell to turn a note on or off.
  • Right-click a cell (on a phone: press and hold) for other kinds of notes: accent, ghost note, flam, drag, cross-stick, buzz, open hi-hat, crash, ride bell…
  • Click a row name (Hi-hat, Snare, Kick…) for actions on the whole bar: all on/off, and “convert to …” to change every note at once (e.g. all accents → normal).
  • TOMS (bottom) shows 4 toms. RIDE shows a separate ride row (a beat holds a hi-hat or a ride, not both).
  • STICKINGS adds a row for R/L stickings.

Bars, time signatures and grids

  • Each bar has its own toolbar. The two first dropdowns set the time signature of that bar (beats / note value), so 4/4, 7/8 and 5/4 can follow each other.
  • The grid is set per beat: 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, triplets or sextuplets. all sets every beat of the bar; GRID in the top bar sets every beat of every bar.
  • 1/32 notes and triplets/sextuplets can't be used in the same groove, and triplets need bars in x/4.
  • + adds a bar: a copy of the last bar or an empty one. The toolbar can move (← →), duplicate, clear or delete a bar.

Listening and looping

  • Play top left, tempo with the BPM slider. The metronome (OFF / 4th / 8th / 16th) is in the top bar.
  • Click a bar in the sheet music to start playback there (green line). Click it again to start from the beginning again.
  • Drag across bars in the sheet music to loop them (orange). Drag the orange edges to change the loop; × stops it. The ▶ and ↻ buttons in a bar's toolbar do the same.
  • The pin (top right of the sheet) keeps the sheet on screen while you scroll.

Sharing, printing, downloading

  • SHARE gives a link (or embed code for a website).
  • PRINT and DOWNLOAD first ask for an optional title, author and comment for on the sheet.
  • Links made with the original Groove Scribe also open in Beatsheet.

Keyboard

  • Space play / stop
  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo
  • ← / → tempo down / up
  • Hold Ctrl and move over cells to turn notes on, Alt to turn them off

Phone

  • It works on a phone, but a full bar is small in portrait. Turn your phone sideways for more room.
